# reference/ — what goes here (and what never does)

This folder holds the **owner's own observations** about what makes the original game fun, so the AI developer can capture the *feeling* without copying anything. The rules come from `docs/25_Original_Game_Reference_Policy.md`.

## Allowed
- `FEEL_NOTES.md` — your notes **in your own words** after playing the original or other games (pacing, tension, what got laughs, what felt slow).
- Notes about **other Roblox games** you like (what their UI, onboarding or shop does well), in your own words.
- Your own sketches, doodles, mood descriptions, and photos you took of real carnivals.
- Concept art and assets **we** made for Pay the Clown!.

## Not allowed (never put these here or give them to the agent)
- Any file from the Gamble With Your Friends install folder (models, textures, sounds, code, data files, configs).
- Screenshots, video clips or recordings of the original game — they pull the agent toward copying its UI layout and art.
- Text copied from the original's in-game descriptions, tooltips, Steam page or wiki pages.
- Ripped or downloaded assets from any other game.

## Why
The Steam Subscriber Agreement forbids reverse engineering, decompiling or deriving from Steam content, and the original's code, art, audio and UI are copyrighted. Ideas and game structure are fine to follow; expression is not. Written feel notes give the agent everything it needs.
